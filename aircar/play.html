📱 Turn your phone sideways for a bigger track!

🕹️ Three buttons

⬆HOP blasts air down. Action and reaction: the air pushes Zip up. Tap to jump, hold to fly higher. Hold it again just before you land for a smooth landing. ➡ZOOM blasts air out the back, and the air pushes Zip forward. Great for big loops. 🌀STICK turns on a fan that sucks air from under Zip, so it's pressed onto the road, even upside down. You can't hop while it's on.

Keyboard: ↑ or Space = Hop, → = Zoom, ↓ or Shift = Stick.

Watch your 💨 air tank: every blast uses air. Grab bubbles and AIR pumps, and Zip's little pump refills it slowly while you drive. Collect 🔩 bolts to upgrade Zip in Rio's Garage.

🔬 Is it real?

The air ideas are real. Action and reaction: push air one way, and the air pushes you the other way. And a strong fan can suck the air from under a car, so the air around holds it on the road. Tesla showed a new Roadster that uses them on October 15, 2026.

Game note: Zip and Dash are pretend cars, and their tracks are much shorter and safer than real ones. Real cars can't do this on normal roads, and only trained engineers handle tanks of squeezed air. Bumps in the game never hurt anyone.
