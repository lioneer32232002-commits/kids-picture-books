🕹️ How to play

  • 🧱 Arch: drag each numbered stone to the slot with the same number, or tap the stone and then the slot. The glowing slots are ready. Build up from both sides; the keystone 🔑 goes last. Then take the frame away!
  • 🔍 Find: tap the pieces in the pool in order, 1, 2, 3... Tap a blue tarp to look underneath.
  • 🌊 Flood: pick the bridge's height (and piers), then press TEST. The flood must pass under it, trucks must get up the ramps, and boats must fit.

On a computer you can drag with the mouse.

🔬 Is it real?

Taipei's Meiji Bridge really opened in 1933 with arches and bronze lamps, and in 2002 it really was taken apart into 435 numbered pieces, which lie in an old swimming pool. Arches really are built on a wooden frame with the keystone last, and bridges over rivers really must let floods pass.

Game note: the arches, floods and numbers are much simpler than in real life, and the flood heights are made up for the game. Where the real bridge will go is still being discussed.
