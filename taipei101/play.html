🕹️ How to play

  • 🌀 Steady: watch the arrow on top of the tower. When the tower moves ▶, hold ◀; when it moves ◀, hold ▶. That swings Bao the other way and calms the tower. Keep the bubble tea from spilling!
  • 🏗️ Build: the crane swings each piece. Tap DROP when it's right above the tower (the little arrow turns green).
  • ⬆️ Elevator: hold ▲ or ▼ to go, let go to slow down. Let go before the 🛑 marker passes the glowing floor.

Keyboard: arrow keys, and Space to drop.

🔬 Is it real?

Taipei 101 really has a 660-tonne golden ball near its top. When wind or an earthquake makes the tower sway, the ball swings the other way and its shock absorbers soak up the motion, so the tower wobbles less. The tower really is made of 8 pieces of 8 floors, and its elevators really go from the 5th to the 89th floor in 37 seconds.

Game note: the real Bao swings all by itself, nobody pushes it. In the game you help it, and the swaying is much bigger and faster than in real life so you can see it. Bao's face is pretend.
