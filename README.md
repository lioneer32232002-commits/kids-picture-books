# Tap & Learn Picture Books

Free, interactive English science picture books for kids aged 5–7.

- **Where Did the Dinosaurs Go?** — `dinosaurs/`
- **Are We There Yet? A Trip to the Planets** — `planets/` + `planets/space.html` (Space Clean-Up! vertical shooter, 8 levels, hangar upgrades)
- **Body Defenders: Veggie Power!** — `immune/` (story) + `immune/play.html` (Phaser 3 tower defense, 8 levels + endless)
- **The Car That Blasts Air** — `aircar/` (action and reaction behind Tesla's 2026 Roadster: hop, zoom, soft landing, suction fan) + `aircar/play.html` (Blast Racer!, Phaser 3 side-view racer with loops, corkscrews and an upside-down road, 8 tracks + Endless Rally)
- **Why Do Airplanes Fly?** — `flight/` (four forces, how a wing makes lift, take-off, thrust, steering and landing with Lin and Poppy) + `flight/play.html` (Sky Pilot!, a kids' cockpit flight simulator: real-style six-pack instruments, stick, throttle, flaps, gear lever; 8 missions + Free Flight)
- **Hello, Paris!** — `paris/` (2,000 years of Paris with Lea and Gus the gargoyle: the Parisii, Notre-Dame, the Louvre, July 14, Haussmann, the Eiffel Tower, the Métro, rebuilding Notre-Dame) + `paris/play.html` (Cathedral Builders!, Phaser 3: stack walls with a crane, place flying buttresses at the push points, put gargoyles on the gutters before the storm; 8 levels + Free Build)
- **The Airport in the Middle of the City** — `songshan/` (Taipei Songshan Airport: 1936 to today, runway numbers and landing into the wind, quiet nights, routes to the islands, private jets, air ambulances and rescue helicopters) + `songshan/play.html` (Sky Rescue!, Phaser 3 top-down rescue flights on the real map of Taiwan's islands with Jetty the jet and Whirly the helicopter: runways and helipads, gear and power lever, storms on the radar, patient care; 8 missions + Free Flight stamps)
- **Here Comes a Typhoon!** — `typhoon/` (how typhoons are born over a warm sea, the counterclockwise spin, the calm eye and the eyewall, size, steering by the Pacific High, Taiwan's typhoon-chasing jet and dropsondes, warnings and getting ready, the eye passing over, the Central Mountain Range, typhoon rain for the reservoirs, with Mei and Swoop) + `typhoon/play.html` (Typhoon Chasers!, Phaser 3: fly around a spinning typhoon whose wind field pushes you, drop dropsondes through rings while staying out of the eyewall and thunderstorms; Get Ready missions where Mei fetches things from the yard before the wind takes them; Typhoon Night with a flashlight and the eye passing; 8 missions + Free Chase)
- **Bepi and Mio's Long Trip to Mercury** — `mercury/` (the BepiColombo mission for kids: Mercury's 88-day year and hot and cold sides, why falling toward the Sun means braking, Bepi and Mio from Europe and Japan, the 2018 launch, gentle ion engines, nine flybys and Giuseppe Colombo, the 2024 engine trouble and new plan, the engine module leaving, arrival planned for 21 November 2026 with a live countdown, Mio separating, ice and the Caloris Basin) + `mercury/play.html` (Mercury Mission!, Phaser 3: flyby missions where gravity speeds you up and FLYBY/BRAKE slow you down until Mercury can catch you; orbit missions over the poles where you take photos without overheating on the day side and release Mio; 8 missions + Free Orbit)
- **Bomi the Black-faced Spoonbill** — `spoonbill/` (a spoonbill's year: the spoon bill and its Taiwanese name, nesting islands off Korea and pink-billed chicks, the autumn flight on the northeast monsoon with a route map, resting by day, catching fish by touch at night, fish farmers draining their ponds, 288 birds in 1989 to 7,746 in the 2026 census, 61% wintering in Taiwan, leg rings, spring yellow feathers, how to watch them kindly) + `spoonbill/play.html` (Spoonbill Journey!, Phaser 3: feed missions where Bomi wades and sweeps in a side-view pond, can't go too deep and scares fish by rushing, with ponds being drained and the tide coming in; fly missions on a map of East Asia riding the monsoon and resting at stopover wetlands; 8 missions + Free Feeding)
- **Emmy's Trip to Phobos** — `phobos/` (Japan's MMX sample-return mission for kids: Mars's two potato-shaped moons, Phobos going around Mars 3 times a Mars day, the October 2026 H3 launch from Tanegashima, the curved trip to Mars, two ideas for where Phobos came from, a jump of hundreds of meters in super-weak gravity, the bouncing Idefix rover, scooping at least 10 g of soil, Stickney crater and Phobos slowly falling toward Mars, the 2031 capsule landing in South Australia, a "how old will you be" timeline) + `phobos/play.html` (Moon Scoop!, Phaser 3: land missions in a side view of Phobos where a fast touchdown bounces you high, then hold SCOOP; rover missions where Idefix hops if it drives fast over bumps; a capsule mission with a parachute line and a landing zone; 8 missions + Free Landing)
- **Bao, the Big Gold Ball of Taipei 101** — `taipei101/` (Taipei 101 for kids: 508 m and 101 floors compared with a house and the Eiffel Tower, 8 bamboo-like pieces of 8 floors, 380 piles 80 m deep, why tall towers sway in the wind, Bao the 660-tonne damper ball, two towers in the same wind with and without Bao, the shock absorbers and the bumper ring, the 1 m swing in Typhoon Soudelor, plates pushing under Taiwan and the April 2024 earthquake, the 37-second elevator ride, New Year's Eve fireworks) + `taipei101/play.html` (Steady Tower!, Phaser 3: steady missions where you swing Bao the other way from the tower's motion to keep the 89th-floor bubble tea from spilling in wind, typhoons and earthquakes; build missions with a swinging crane; elevator missions where you let go in time to stop at the floor; 8 missions + Endless Storm)
- **The Bridge in 435 Pieces** — `meiji/` (Taipei's Meiji Bridge for kids: the 1901 iron bridge and the 1933 arch bridge with bronze lamps, how an arch stands with the keystone last, the bridge's timeline to the 2027 report, Typhoon Nari and why bridges must let floods pass (an interactive flood test using the game's own rules), taking it apart in 2002, why every piece has a number, the 435 pieces in an old swimming pool, ideas for where it could go, the 2026 workshop) + `meiji/play.html` (Bridge Puzzle!, Phaser 3: arch missions where you drag numbered stones onto a wooden frame from both sides with the keystone last, then take the frame away and add the bronze lamps; find missions where you tap the pieces in the pool in order and lift tarps; flood missions where you pick the bridge's height and piers and test it with a flood, a truck and a boat; 8 missions + Free Build)
- **Rivers in the Sea** — `currents/` (ocean currents around Taiwan, with a season map) + `currents/play.html` (Ride the Current!, Phaser 3 flow-field swimming game, 8 trips + Endless Kuroshio)

Live: https://lioneer32232002-commits.github.io/kids-picture-books/

Art style, characters, writing and game-design rules: see **`STYLE.md`**.

## Structure

| Path | What |
|------|------|
| `assets/book.js`, `assets/book.css` | Shared engine: paging, swipe, read-aloud (Web Speech), synthesized sound effects (Web Audio), particle FX (fire, explosions, ash/snow/stars weather) |
| `dinosaurs/`, `planets/` | One `index.html` per book + `img/` (WebP pages and cut-out sprites) |
| `banners/` | Facebook banners: `*-fb.jpg` 1200×630 (link/ad), `*-square.jpg` 1080×1080 (feed post) |
| `tools/process_images.py` | Raw Gemini images in `_raw/` (not committed) → WebP pages + transparent sprites |
| `immune/game.js` | Tower defense game (Phaser 3 from jsDelivr). `?unlock=1` unlocks all levels; `?timer=1` runs the loop on setTimeout for automated testing |
| `planets/space.js` | Space Clean-Up! shooter (Phaser 3). Same `?unlock=1` / `?timer=1` test flags. Friendly aliens can't be hit by design |
| `currents/geo.js` | Simplified map of Taiwan's seas (coastlines, currents by season, places) shared by the book's SVG map and the game's mini-map |
| `currents/game.js` | Ride the Current! (Phaser 3). The sea is a flow field (current lanes + eddies) that moves Mo and everything floating. `?unlock=1`, `?timer=1`, `?speed=3` (test runs) |
| `aircar/sim.js` | Blast Racer! physics and tracks with no drawing (runs in Node too): the car rides a track surface or flies; loops only hold you if you're fast or sticking. Also the bot that sets Dash's racing line |
| `aircar/game.js` | Blast Racer! scenes (Phaser 3). `?unlock=1`, `?timer=1`, `?speed=3`. `tools/dev/puff_autopilot.js` steps it frame by frame |
| `flight/sim.js` | Sky Pilot! flight physics, islands, runways and missions with no drawing (runs in Node). Lift = speed² × wing tilt, flaps, gear drag, a stall-protecting fly-by-wire stick. Also the autopilot bot |
| `flight/game.js` | Sky Pilot! scenes (Phaser 3). Hand-made 3D: a camera projects sea, islands, runways and billboards (clouds, cloud rings, trees, balloons); the cockpit panel is drawn on top. `?unlock=1`, `?timer=1`, `?speed=3`. `node tools/dev/flight_autopilot.js` flies every mission |
| `paris/sim.js` | Cathedral Builders! rules and shapes with no drawing (runs in Node): levels, push points, gutters, scoring, and the bot used for balancing |
| `paris/game.js` | Cathedral Builders! scenes (Phaser 3). `?unlock=1`, `?timer=1`, `?speed=3`, `?bot=1` (the sim's bot plays). `tools/dev/cathedral_autopilot.js` steps it frame by frame |
| `songshan/sim.js` | Sky Rescue! world, aircraft, missions and bot with no drawing (runs in Node). Uses `currents/geo.js` projected at 1,500 px per degree; runways have real headings and numbers, jets land into the wind through an approach funnel, helicopters land on helipads |
| `songshan/game.js` | Sky Rescue! scenes (Phaser 3). Map details are drawn into 600 px chunks hidden when off screen. `?unlock=1`, `?timer=1`, `?speed=3`; `window.__srBot = true` lets the bot fly. `node tools/dev/rescue_autopilot.js` flies every mission |
| `typhoon/sim.js` | Typhoon Chasers! rules with no drawing (runs in Node): the wind field (counterclockwise, calm eye, strongest at the eyewall, a little inflow), the storm moving along its track, drop rings and thunderstorm cells, the Get Ready helpers (Mei walks out to the yard, Grandpa takes the balcony), and the bot. `node tools/dev/typhoon_autopilot.js` plays every mission (`all slow` = a slower kid) |
| `typhoon/game.js` | Typhoon Chasers! scenes (Phaser 3): chase map + a separate HUD scene (mini-map, forecast cone, fuel), the house for Get Ready, lights-out with a flashlight, the eye. `?unlock=1`, `?timer=1`, `?speed=3`; `window.__tcBot = true` lets the bot play |
| `mercury/sim.js` | Mercury Mission! rules with no drawing (runs in Node): flyby missions keep energy E = v²/2 − G/r along a looping path from Earth's orbit to Mercury's (flybys and the brake take energy away, too fast at Mercury adds another loop); orbit missions over the poles with height, heat on the day side, photo spots and releasing Mio; and the bot. `node tools/dev/mercury_autopilot.js` plays every mission (`all sloppy` = late or early flyby taps) |
| `mercury/game.js` | Mercury Mission! scenes (Phaser 3): the inner Solar System from above with a separate HUD scene (speed gauge with the arrival speed, FLYBY, BRAKE), and Mercury from the side. Planet sprites come from `planets/img`. Stars are one tiled texture, not thousands of dots (that made the flyby view run at 10 fps). `?unlock=1`, `?timer=1`, `?speed=3`; `window.__mmBot = true` lets the bot play |
| `spoonbill/sim.js` | Spoonbill Journey! rules and map with no drawing (runs in Node): hand-simplified coastlines of East Asia in longitude and latitude (also drawn on page 4 of the book), the feed rules (pond floor, water level, wading depth, sweeping bill, fish that flee fast steps), the fly rules (monsoon wind, energy, stopovers, rain clouds) and the bot. `node tools/dev/spoonbill_autopilot.js` plays every mission (`all hasty` = a kid who rushes) |
| `spoonbill/game.js` | Spoonbill Journey! scenes (Phaser 3): the feed view draws the water and mud over a painted background; the fly view is the map with wind streaks, a mini-map and the energy bar. `?unlock=1`, `?timer=1`, `?speed=3`; `window.__sjBot = true` lets the bot play |
| `phobos/sim.js` | Moon Scoop! rules with no drawing (runs in Node): the lander (weak gravity, thrusters, fuel and one reserve tank, soft-touchdown limits, bounces off the ground and boulders, scooping), the rover (hops when the ground drops away faster than gravity can pull it down, then rights itself), the capsule (Earth gravity, air drag, parachute line, wind) and the bot. `node tools/dev/phobos_autopilot.js` plays every mission (`all rough` = a kid who pushes too hard and drives too fast) |
| `phobos/game.js` | Moon Scoop! scenes (Phaser 3) with hold-down buttons (several at once on touch screens): Land and Rover scroll sideways over the painted Mars sky; Home is a tall sky from black space to the desert picture. `?unlock=1`, `?timer=1`, `?speed=3`; `window.__msBot = true` lets the bot play |
| `taipei101/sim.js` | Steady Tower! rules with no drawing (runs in Node): the tower's sway (one swing every 3 s, wind gusts at the same rhythm, earthquakes) with extra damping when Bao is swung against the tower's motion, the bubble-tea slosh meter, the crane and stacking tolerance, the elevator (60 km/h top speed, braking distance, self-leveling near the floor) and the bot. `node tools/dev/tower_autopilot.js` plays every mission (`all late` = a kid who reacts late, `all lazy` = one who doesn't press anything) |
| `taipei101/game.js` | Steady Tower! scenes (Phaser 3): the tower is drawn as polygons that bend most at the top, with Bao in a round window; the crane and stack scroll up as the tower grows; the elevator shaft scrolls past the cab with a 🛑 "where you'd stop" marker. `?unlock=1`, `?timer=1`, `?speed=3`; `window.__stBot = true` lets the bot play |
| `meiji/sim.js` | Bridge Puzzle! rules with no drawing (runs in Node, and the book's flood page uses it too): arch slots that open from the feet up (keystone last), the find order with tarps, the flood check (flood height plus 0.3 m per pier, freeboard, 8% ramps, 40 m longest arch, room for a boat) and the bot. `node tools/dev/bridge_autopilot.js` plays every mission (`all kid` = a kid who makes a few mistakes) |
| `meiji/game.js` | Bridge Puzzle! scenes (Phaser 3): drag (or tap, then tap) stones from a tray onto wedge-shaped slots; the pool with numbered blocks and tarps; the river from the side with a bridge you raise and lower, a flood, a truck and a boat. Every tap goes through the sim's step, so its messages are never lost. `?unlock=1`, `?timer=1`, `?speed=3`; `window.__bpBot = true` lets the bot play |
| `tools/dev/` | Helpers: Gemini in-page capture + tile stitching (when Chrome blocks downloads), contact sheets, auto-play bots for balancing the games (`current_autopilot.js` steps the game frame by frame, so it works in hidden tabs) |
| `tools/make_banners.py` | Renders banners from `tools/banners/banner.html` with headless Chrome (needs `python -m http.server 8080`). `python tools/make_banners.py currents` renders one book only |

Illustrations generated with Google Gemini.
