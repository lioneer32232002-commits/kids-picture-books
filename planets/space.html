🚀Space Clean-Up!
📖 Story

🕹️ How to fly

Drag anywhere (or use the arrow keys) to move Nova's rocket. The laser fires all by itself! Clean up junk and rocks, grab ✨ stardust, and reach the planet. Watch for the red ⚠ warning: comets are too big to clean, so dodge them!

🛠️ Your gear

Laser power-up: up to 5 beams at once. Bolt the robot dog: a helper that aims for you (up to 2). Shield bubble: blocks one bump. Magnet: pulls in stardust for 10 seconds. Stardust: charges 💥 Star Burst and buys upgrades in the Hangar.

👽 Be kind in space

Friendly aliens are living things, so the cleaner beam passes right through them. Fly over and say hi to make a new friend!

Science note: real asteroids are about a million kilometers apart, and real scientists plan to catch space junk with nets and robot arms, because breaking it apart makes more junk. The game makes space busier so it's fun to play!
