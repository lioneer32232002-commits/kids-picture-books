✈️ A jet plane would take about 18 days. 🚀 Our super rocket: only about 7 hours!

Pick a planet, then blast off!

Now let's go farther! Pick a planet and a ride, and the spaceship computer will tell you how long the trip takes.

A long trip means you get hungry. Astronauts must pack all their food before they leave. There are no shops in space!

Some trips are so long that you'd get bored... and you'd need mountains of food. What if you could sleep the whole way, like a bear in winter?

🔬 True or pretend? Pretend, for now! People can't be frozen and woken up yet. Scientists study how bears hibernate to make "space sleep" possible one day.

Here's the tricky part. While you travel, time keeps going on Earth. Your family keeps getting older, even if you were asleep in the pod.

How old are you?

Home Sweet Home

Space is amazing, but it's really, really far. That's why scientists keep building faster spaceships, and why we send brave robots to explore for us.

And Earth? It's the only planet we know where we can breathe the air, splash in the sea, and eat pizza with our family. 💛
